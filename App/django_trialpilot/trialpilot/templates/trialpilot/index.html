📖 About the Application

This platform is designed to support oncology clinicians by transforming unstructured clinical data into structured information and enabling efficient clinical trial matching and outcome analysis.

📂 Clinical Diaries

Upload and manage unstructured clinical diaries. These documents serve as the input for the extraction pipeline, where relevant patient information is identified and processed.

👤 Patient Profiles

View structured patient data extracted from clinical diaries, including demographics, diagnosis, staging, and treatment history. These profiles are validated and stored for further analysis.

🧪 Clinical Trials

Import and manage clinical trial documents. The system extracts inclusion and exclusion criteria and converts them into logical rules that can be validated by clinicians.

✅ Eligibility Results

Automatically match patients with clinical trials using deterministic evaluation. The system provides a list of eligible patients along with detailed justifications for inclusion or exclusion.

📊 Analysis Dashboard

Analyze patient outcomes using survival metrics such as Overall Survival (OS) and Progression-Free Survival (PFS), including Kaplan–Meier visualizations and statistical summaries.

By integrating Large Language Models with clinician validation and deterministic logic, the system reduces manual workload, improves transparency, and supports data-driven decision-making in oncology.
