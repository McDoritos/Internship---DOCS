CLINICAL STYLE VARIATION

Each diary should simulate being written by a different clinician.

Allow variation in:
- sentence length
- abbreviation usage
- level of detail
- structure of sections
- exam description style

IMPERFECTION DISTRIBUTION

Imperfections should appear in different parts of the diary.

They may appear:
- inside clinical history
- inside laboratory results
- between sentences
- inside examination descriptions

Avoid concentrating all imperfections only at the end of the document.

REALISM TRANSFORMATION RULES

Introduce a SMALL number of realistic documentation imperfections.
The goal is subtle realism, not heavy corruption.

Formatting inconsistencies (choose a few)
- uneven spacing between words
- inconsistent spacing before or after punctuation
- irregular line breaks
- slightly misaligned blocks of text
- abrupt spacing shifts between paragraphs

Capitalization inconsistencies
- occasional uppercase word
- mixed capitalization in section labels
- inconsistent use of abbreviations (e.g., ECOG-PS vs ECOG-ps)

Minor spelling imperfections
- introduce 1–3 mild spelling errors
- examples: missing accent, slightly distorted medical word
- do not create unreadable text

Structural asymmetry
- occasionally remove a header
- allow a clinical block to appear without a clear label
- slightly alter the order of one or two sections

Natural repetition
Repeat some clinically relevant information later in the note without explanation.

Examples:
- ECOG
- clinical stage
- treatment line
- therapy name

The repetition must appear naturally within the text and must not be explained.

Fragmented report excerpts
You may insert a short fragment of an imaging or diagnostic report.

Fragments may appear incomplete but must remain clinically plausible.

Clinical workflow noise (optional but encouraged)
Short administrative or workflow notes may appear between clinical statements.

Examples of realistic workflow fragments:
- Discutido em reunião multidisciplinar.
- Pedido enviado à CFT.
- Aguarda marcação RT.
- Consulta HDIA agendada.

SHORT INTERRUPTION LINES
